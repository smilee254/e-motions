How to use e-motions

A private space to share what's on your mind.

About e-motions

e-motions is a peer-to-peer support platform for Kenyans. You can talk anonymously with another person, or with Sentinel, our AI listener, when no peers are available. We do not use accounts, and we do not track you.

Guide

Enter

Tap the enter button. e-motions connects you to the network without requiring any sign-up or personal information.

Match with a peer or Sentinel

If another person is online, you will connect to them anonymously. If not, Sentinel AI will listen and respond without judgment.

Share

Type whatever is on your mind into the text box. There are no timers or requirements.

AI Transfer

If you feel uncomfortable during a peer chat, you can transfer to Sentinel AI at any time.

senti Type this word and press Enter

You will be silently transferred to Sentinel AI. The peer will see a note that you have left the chat. Sentinel will not ask questions.

Quick Escape

If you need to hide the screen immediately:

  • Tap the door icon in the top left corner, OR
  • Press the Escape (ESC) key 3 times quickly.

You will be redirected to Google, leaving no trace of the chat.

Features

Voice Notes

Hold the microphone icon to record a voice note. The system translates your voice into text and deletes the audio.

Quiet Mode

Tap the eye icon in the top left to dim the interface.

Journal Mode

Type /journal to make Sentinel a silent listener. Type /done when finished.
