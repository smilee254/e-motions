User Manual

How to use e-motions

A quiet, safe space to share what's on your mind — here's everything you need to know.

🌱 What is e-motions?

e-motions is a peer-to-peer emotional support platform built for Kenyans. You can talk anonymously with a fellow traveler (real person) or with Sentinel — our warm AI companion — whenever no peers are available. No names, no accounts, total privacy.

🗺️ Step-by-Step Guide

1

Click "Enter the Sanctuary"

Tap the button on the home screen. e-motions will quietly connect you to the network — no sign-up, no email, no passwords needed. Ever.

2

You'll be matched with a peer or Sentinel

If another person is online nearby, you'll be connected to them anonymously. If no one is available right now, Sentinel AI (call him Senti 😊) will hold space for you — warm, judgment-free, always present.

3

Share freely — at your own pace

Type whatever is on your mind into the text box at the bottom. There's no timer, no pressure. You can share a little or a lot. The chat is your space.

4

Rate Sentinel's responses

When Sentinel replies, you'll see a 👍 / 👎 button. Your feedback helps Sentinel grow and get better at supporting people like you. It takes one second and matters a lot.

🛡️ The "Senti" Safety Switch

Feeling overwhelmed or uncomfortable during a peer chat? You are never trapped. At any moment, simply type:

senti and press Enter / Send

You will be instantly and silently transferred to Sentinel AI. The peer will only see a gentle note that you've stepped away. Sentinel will immediately understand you were in a conversation that felt uncomfortable and will open with warmth and care — no questions asked, no judgment.

💡 This works at any time, even mid-sentence. Your safety always comes first.

🚪 Quick Escape Panic Button

If you are in an unsafe physical environment and someone approaches while you are chatting, you can instantly hide the screen.

  • Tap the door icon in the top left corner, OR
  • Press the Escape (ESC) key 3 times quickly on your keyboard.

You will be immediately redirected to Google, leaving no trace of the chat behind.

🛠️ Sanctuary Features

🎤

Anonymous Voice Notes

Too tired to type? Hold the microphone icon next to the text box to record a voice note. The system instantly translates your voice into text and destroys the audio, keeping you completely anonymous.

👁️

Quiet Mode

If you're feeling sensory overload, tap the eye icon in the top left. This dims the interface to a deep, dark calm and stops all moving animations.

📖

Private Journal Mode

If you just want to dump your thoughts without Senti trying to give advice, type /journal. Senti will become a silent listener. When you are finished, type /done.

✨ Tips for the Best Experience

  • Be honest. The more you share, the more Sentinel and your peers can support you.
  • Be kind. The person on the other end is a real human going through their own journey. Kindness is mandatory here.
  • Use the dark/light toggle (top-right sun/moon icon) for a comfortable reading experience at any hour.
  • No phone numbers or personal info. The platform will gently remind you if you accidentally share private details.

We protect this space fiercely. Kindness is mandatory. Hate speech, bullying, or deliberately hurting others will result in an immediate ban. We're here to hold each other up — not tear each other down.
